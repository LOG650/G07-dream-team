# Project References: G07 - Dream Team (LOG650)

This document contains direct links and summaries for the academic sources and reports cited in the final project report.

## 1. Academic Papers

### Breiman, L. (2001)

**Title:** Random forests

- **Journal:** Machine Learning
- **Direct Link:** [https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1010933404324](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1010933404324)
- **Summary:** Introduces the original Random Forest algorithm, which forms the core machine learning methodology for predictive risk mapping and routing decisions in the project.

### Choi, T. M. (2020)

**Title:** Innovative “Bring-Service-Near-Your-Home” operations under Corona-Virus (COVID-19/SARS-CoV-2) outbreak: Can logistics become the Messiah?

- **Journal:** Transportation Research Part E: Logistics and Transportation Review
- **Direct Link:** [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tre.2020.101961](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tre.2020.101961)
- **Summary:** Explores mobile service operation (MSO) models developed during disruptions to maintain business continuity through innovative logistics, real-time response, and adaptive technology.

### Hosseini, S., Ivanov, D., & Dolgui, A. (2019)

**Title:** Review of quantitative methods for supply chain resilience analysis

- **Journal:** Transportation Research Part E: Logistics and Transportation Review
- **Direct Link:** [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tre.2019.03.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tre.2019.03.001)
- **Summary:** A systematic review of quantitative modeling for supply chain resilience, focusing on the categorization of absorptive, adaptive, and restorative capacities (e.g., backup suppliers).

### Ivanov, D. (2021)

**Title:** Supply Chain Viability and the COVID-19 pandemic: a conceptual and formal generalisation of four major adaptation strategies

- **Journal:** International Journal of Production Research
- **Direct Link:** [https://doi.org/10.1080/00207543.2021.1890852](https://doi.org/10.1080/00207543.2021.1890852)
- **Summary:** Outlines the concept of supply chain viability, ecosystem adaptation, and resilience frameworks under long-term structural disruptions. Pages 3535–3552.

### Ivanov, D., Sokolov, B., & Dolgui, A. (2014)

**Title:** The Ripple effect in supply chains: trade-off ‘efficiency-flexibility-resilience’ in disruption management

- **Journal:** International Journal of Production Research
- **Direct Link:** [https://doi.org/10.1080/00207543.2013.858836](https://doi.org/10.1080/00207543.2013.858836)
- **Summary:** Introduces the concept of the "Ripple Effect" and analyzes the essential trade-offs between cost-optimization, system flexibility, and resilience.

### Ponomarov, S. Y., & Holcomb, M. C. (2009)

**Title:** Understanding the concept of supply chain resilience

- **Journal:** The International Journal of Logistics Management
- **Direct Link:** [https://doi.org/10.1108/09574090910954873](https://doi.org/10.1108/09574090910954873)
- **Summary:** Provides an integrated perspective on resilience across multiple disciplines, defining the adaptive capability and recovery functions of supply networks.

### Sheffi, Y., & Rice, J. B. (2005)

**Title:** A supply chain view of the resilient enterprise

- **Journal:** MIT Sloan Management Review
- **Direct Link:** [https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/a-supply-chain-view-of-the-resilient-enterprise/](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/a-supply-chain-view-of-the-resilient-enterprise/)
- **Summary:** Establishes the classic disruption and recovery profile, illustrating how organizations plan for, absorb, and rebound from operational shocks.

### Simon, H. A. (1955)

**Title:** A behavioral model of rational choice

- **Journal:** The Quarterly Journal of Economics
- **Direct Link:** [https://doi.org/10.2307/1884852](https://doi.org/10.2307/1884852)
- **Summary:** Framework for bounded rationality, supporting the project's premise that automated decision-support systems are required when human managers face information overload during disruptions.

## 2. Institutional Reports and Datasets

### Gedipudi, L. (2026)

**Title:** Supply Chain Disruption and Recovery Dataset

- **Type:** Data set
- **Direct Link:** [https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/likithagedipudi/supply-chain-disruption-and-recovery-dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/likithagedipudi/supply-chain-disruption-and-recovery-dataset)
- **Summary:** Publicly available shipment and operational dataset used to construct, test, and validate the tactical re-allocation routing algorithms.

### IATA (2026)

**Title:** Air Cargo Market Analysis: Air Cargo Volumes Rise Despite Network Turmoil (April 2026)

- **Publisher:** International Air Transport Association
- **Direct Link:** [https://www.iata.org/en/iata-repository/publications/economic-reports/air-cargo-market-analysis-april-2026/](https://www.iata.org/en/iata-repository/publications/economic-reports/air-cargo-market-analysis-april-2026/)
- **Summary:** Reports critical market data as of April 2026, documenting a 121.1% surge in jet fuel prices and 32.2% increase in cargo yields, providing real-world validation for the project's cost-sensitivity parameters.

### Uskono, B. M. (2026)

**Title:** Global Supply Chain Disruption & Resilience

- **Type:** Data set
- **Direct Link:** [https://doi.org/10.34740/KAGGLE/DSV/14387044](https://doi.org/10.34740/KAGGLE/DSV/14387044)
- **Summary:** Recent historical and simulated disruption dataset used for training the Random Forest risk-classification model.

### World Bank (2023)

**Title:** Logistics Performance Index (LPI) 2023: Connecting to Compete

- **Publisher:** The World Bank
- **Direct Link:** [https://lpi.worldbank.org/](https://lpi.worldbank.org/)
- **Summary:** Used for cross-border infrastructure benchmarking, country risk scoring, and calculating baseline lead times for maritime and air transportation networks.
