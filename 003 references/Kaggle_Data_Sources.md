# Kaggle Data Sources

The project utilizes the following datasets for analysis and model training.

## 1. Supply Chain Disruption and Recovery Dataset
- **Author:** Gedipudi, L. (2026)
- **URL:** [https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/likithagedipudi/supply-chain-disruption-and-recovery-dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/likithagedipudi/supply-chain-disruption-and-recovery-dataset)
- **Contents:** Historical data on disruption events, types, and recovery durations across various industries.

## 2. Global Supply Chain Disruption & Resilience
- **Author:** Uskono, B. M. (2026)
- **URL:** [https://doi.org/10.34740/KAGGLE/DSV/14387044](https://doi.org/10.34740/KAGGLE/DSV/14387044)
- **Contents:** Global logistics data including lead times, shipping costs, and risk indices used for stress-testing and decision modeling.
